Design and constraints

Compact authoring for church editors, preserving Builder’s green accent, Arial typography, existing light/dark themes and native controls. Design variance 2/10; motion 1/10; density 8/10. Remove repeated navigation and expanded editors before reducing control size.

Routine gutters at most 16px, panels at most 12px, related gaps 8–12px, major gaps 16px. Body/control text at least 14px. Touch editing text at least 16px; frequent touch targets 44px. Authored site CSS, data, breakpoints, images, rendering and publication rules are unchanged.

No new dependency, schema, preference mode or editor engine. React/Puck/CSS remain the stack. Current AGENTS.md supersedes the Issue’s historical Cloudflare release instructions: Canary first, exact candidate approval before Production, then Production acceptance.
