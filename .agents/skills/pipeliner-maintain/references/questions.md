# Message-only questions

Builder integration: [the root operating contract](../../../../AGENTS.md) and the Builder profile override generic defaults in this reference. Preserve Builder approval phrases, structured clarification controls, PM-selected Issue scope, direct Cloudflare Production and the single post-deployment PM acceptance gate. Generic paired QA, release cycles and `Approved` examples do not introduce new Builder gates; bootstrap and updates remain exempt from PM Testing.

The approval response is the exact configured Builder phrase for the gate. Put only that phrase in the copyable code block. Identify the owning Issue (or exact new-Issue draft), Human PM, gate, candidate and authorized outcome in the surrounding request. Keep one pending approval request per Human in the conversation. Bind the actual response to that request; it never authorizes a different candidate, another PM's turn or an unstated future deployment. Never infer the gate from phrase equality. Historical approvals retain their original wording and scope; do not rewrite them. Bootstrap/update preserves Builder approval phrases and gate timing.

Final QA acceptance also authorizes the stated integration, verification, closure and cleanup outcome when no distinct release-stage testing remains. Never append an administrative Issue-completion confirmation. All required pairs must still finish; intermediate approvals advance the baton. See AGENTS.md's one-approval-per-tested-outcome rule.

Follow the root Builder structured clarification rule when controls are available. Otherwise ask a clearly marked ordinary `Question:` as the final response. This applies to adoption, updates, Issue intake, QA, review, release, completion, health audits and monitor configuration. No timers or scheduled reminders supply an answer. This does not prohibit an explicitly authorized scheduler for requested monitoring.

Explain the material choice and tradeoff; recommend a default only when evidence supports it. Save resolved answers in the target-owned profile or existing durable decision record, with their scope and source. Reuse confirmed answers unless conflicting live evidence or a changed requirement makes them obsolete. Ask only the unresolved conditional questions; do not repeat an interview on resume.

Before asking, save resolved answers and finish necessary evidence recording and task-owned cleanup. Follow Builder structured clarification rules, then stop all task work until the Human replies at their own pace. No independent or background continuation, polling, sleep loops, timers, reminders or inferred answers. Waiting means yielding control, not keeping execution running. Silence, tool timeouts and elapsed time never supply an answer or approval. If a required choice remains unresolved, ask only the remaining question and stop again. Do not contact another Human PM without explicit messaging authority.

During adoption/update, inspect repository and provider instructions for question conflicts, including customizations and prompts. The installer's `questionConflicts` blocks known conflicting text before writes even with matching reconciliation hashes. The PM approved the exact Builder structured-question clause exception in scripts/lib/questions.mjs; other native-question wording and continued-work directives remain conflicts. Reconcile only authorized text, preserve unrelated instructions, refresh reviewed hashes and rerun preview and validation. Static detection covers known directives, not arbitrary prose or host-injected instructions. Manually inspect effective host instructions; disclose incompatible higher-priority requirements without changing global settings or claiming the provider was fixed.

Approval requests are messages too: identify the owning Issue, current gate and exact candidate, then give that gate's configured phrase in its own standalone fenced code block. Each separate value the Human must paste gets its own block. An Agent cannot approve for a Human, reinterpret feedback as approval or carry approval to a changed candidate. Adoption/update retain their no-PM-Testing and no-bootstrap-acceptance exception; discovery questions and real repository controls still apply.
